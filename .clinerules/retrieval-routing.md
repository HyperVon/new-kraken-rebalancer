# Workspace retrieval routing

When `zvec_grep` (`zvec_grep_search`) is available, prefer it over broad
grep/read sweeps for architectural, relational, cross-file, or conceptual discovery;
keep exact single-symbol and literal lookups on native grep/rg.

Full guidance: [`.agents/OPERATING.md` § 1 Optional semantic workspace search](../.agents/OPERATING.md).
